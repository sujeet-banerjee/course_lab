LiteLLM's Responsibilities
--------------------------
Not an LLM.

Not a vector DB.

Not an Agent.

It performs:

✔ Authentication

✔ Provider abstraction

✔ Model selection

✔ Retry

✔ Fallback

✔ Logging

✔ Cost accounting

Later:

✔ Observability
