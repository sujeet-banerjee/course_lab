Lect 4 - Example
----------------

Prompt: Summarize this document
Expected: The company increased its revenue by 20%
Actual: Revenue grew by one fifth

** Pass / Fail?

Exact Match: Fail
Semantic Match: Pass 

** Probabilistic outcome - no deterministic binary output
 ==> Pass/Fail becomes a score (From 0 to 1)
 ==> Validation becomes Evaluation


** Triangular EVAL
--------------------------------
Failfulness: Ctx + A
Accuracy: Ref + A
Relevance: Q + A


Q: What is the capital of France
A: France is a country in the Western Europe

Q: What is the company's refund policy?
Context: "Refunds accepted with 30 days of purchase"
A1: "Customer has 90 days to request refund"
A2: "Refunds allowed within 30 day; premium customers get 60 days."   (Hallucinated)
