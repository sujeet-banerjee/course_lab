Slide 1: CoT
------------
- Logical division of tasks into sub-tasks: step-by-step (sequential or parallel)
- Reasoning: It's chain of cause and effect.
- Example: how will you compute 2 * 3+4 * 5? 
       --> say, Solve in two steps: 
              STEP1: first multiply or divide, 
              STEP2: second add the results obtained from previous step.
- Breaking this is often a 'divide and conquer' strategy, and gives the AI better reasoning capability.
- You are forcing the AI to think into logical steps - so it remove some level of non-determinism.
