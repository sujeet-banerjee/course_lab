No new knowledge enters automatically.

Why I recommend this approach

Many RAG tutorials start with embeddings and vector databases, but students often don't appreciate why those components exist. By spending a full session establishing the problem—showing that even a sophisticated agent with memory, tools, and routing cannot answer questions about unseen enterprise documents—you create a clear need for the retrieval pipeline. When you introduce embeddings on Day 16, students will see them as the solution to a problem they've already experienced, rather than just another technical concept. This makes the entire Week 3 progression much more intuitive and memorable.
