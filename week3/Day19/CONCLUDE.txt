Students should understand that retrieval is an optimization problem with competing objectives:

Increase recall → retrieve more documents (top_k ↑).
Increase precision → apply metadata filters and reranking.
Minimize latency and cost → reduce the candidate set.
Maintain robustness → avoid filters that are so strict they exclude the correct answer.

That's the mindset architects use when designing RAG systems, and it's a much more enduring lesson than any particular workflow configuration.
